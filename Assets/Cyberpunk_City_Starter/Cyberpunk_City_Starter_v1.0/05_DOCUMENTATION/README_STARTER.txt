Cyberpunk City Starter Pack — Free Pixel Art Assets
Creator: Burcu Sak
Version: 1.0 Starter
Release: 2026

ABOUT
This is a small free sampler from the Cyberpunk City Asset Pack.
It is designed to let game developers test the art style and import workflow
before purchasing the Complete Edition.

INCLUDED
- 2 sample cyberpunk structures
- 8 sample props
- 20 individual 16x16 sample tiles
- 1 complete drone animation sprite sheet
- 1 GIF preview of the drone animation
- Starter license

IMPORTANT
This Starter Pack intentionally does NOT include the full parallax background set,
full tilesets, full city environment, full animation library, demo city sources,
or editable Aseprite master files.

FULL COMPLETE EDITION
For the full city environment, 597 individual tiles, layered parallax backgrounds,
15 animation sprite sheets, demo scenes and editable Aseprite source files:

https://uniqman.itch.io/cyberpunk-city-asset-pack-complete

PIXEL-ART IMPORT SETTINGS
- Texture filtering: OFF
- Interpolation: OFF
- Scaling: nearest-neighbor
- Integer scaling is recommended when possible
